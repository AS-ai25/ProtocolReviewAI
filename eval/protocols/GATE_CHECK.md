## Reading the table

* `NCT in text = no` means the document itself does not cite its registry number (the id comes from the file name); the rule MISSING_REGISTRY_ID fires and the registry cross-check uses the id from the file name.
* The scientific rule hits are regex screens, not findings: they are the starting point of the label drafts in `eval/labeled_cases/_drafts/` and must be confirmed or deleted by the labeler against the PDF.
* Short documents (< 10 pages) are synopsis-level protocols; whether they stay in the evaluation set is the labeler's call (see SOURCES.md).
