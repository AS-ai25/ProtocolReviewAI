# ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice - key protocol expectations (condensed)

Reference codes `GCP-nn` used in findings. Condensed by the team from ICH
E6(R3) (2025) principles and the protocol-content expectations carried over
from E6(R2) §6. These GCP codes are project-defined review categories, not official ICH clause numbers; consult the cited guideline for authoritative wording.

- GCP-01 Trials are conducted in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki and applicable regulations; ethics committee approval before start.
- GCP-02 Foreseeable risks and inconveniences are weighed against anticipated benefit; the protocol must contain an explicit risk/benefit assessment.
- GCP-03 Rights, safety and well-being of participants prevail over interests of science and society; vulnerable populations need additional safeguards.
- GCP-04 Adequate non-clinical and clinical information on the investigational product supports the proposed trial (investigator's brochure referenced for drug trials).
- GCP-05 Scientifically sound protocol: clear objectives, design, methodology, statistical considerations and organisation; quality by design with critical-to-quality factors identified.
- GCP-06 Investigational product: description, dosage, dosing regimen, packaging, labelling, accountability and storage conditions.
- GCP-07 Safety: definitions of adverse events / serious adverse events, timelines for expedited reporting to sponsor, ethics committee and regulators; procedures for follow-up.
- GCP-08 Data integrity: source documents, case report forms, audit trails, data handling and record retention periods.
- GCP-09 Informed consent: process, information to be given, documentation, consent of legally acceptable representatives, assent for minors, re-consent after amendments.
- GCP-10 Quality management and monitoring: risk-based monitoring plan, auditing, deviations handling; sponsor oversight of delegated activities.
- GCP-11 Protocol deviations and amendments: definition, documentation and approval workflow.
- GCP-12 Direct access to source data for monitors, auditors, ethics committees and regulators.


## Applicability and implementation notes (review addendum, 2026-11-04)

The twelve project-specific `GCP-01` to `GCP-12` identifiers above are **internal project labels**, not official numbered clauses of ICH E6(R3). Their original order and wording are preserved. The underlying final ICH E6(R3) Principles and Annex 1 are an authoritative source for clinical trials within their scope, but local Israeli implementation and trial category must be checked separately. This appendix is an evidence-based crosswalk, **not a certified line-by-line legal validation**.

- **GCP-01 to GCP-03 (ethics/participants):** Link risk-benefit justification, protection of participants who may be vulnerable in a particular context, scientific value and independent ethics review. Pregnancy, minors and impaired decision-making capacity require appropriate safeguards and an applicable consent/assent procedure, not automatic exclusion.
- **GCP-04 to GCP-06 (design/intervention):** Review whether product and prior evidence supports the proposed study. If no investigational product is involved (retrospective, questionnaires), label product-only expectations as N/A; do not manufacture product findings. ICH E8(R1) adds proportional, critical-to-quality planning.
- **GCP-07 and GCP-10 (safety/monitoring):** Harms reporting, investigator/sponsor oversight and risk-based monitoring should fit the interventions and risks. The absence of a specific DMC may be justified. Avoid declaring a reporting deadline without verifying jurisdiction and event category.
- **GCP-08 and GCP-12 (information governance):** Check source-data traceability, controlled access, audit trails, safeguards, transfer, storage/retention and access for oversight under applicable permissions. A code-to-identity lookup key means data remain re-identifiable; distinguish pseudonymization from anonymity. A password alone does not establish adequate governance. Cross-border cloud storage merits explicit responsibilities and controls, not an automatic prohibition.
- **GCP-09 (consent):** Describe who obtains consent, how it is recorded and special-population safeguards; for retrospective data, document the legal/IRB basis for consent waiver where allowed. The absence of an ICF appendix from a public protocol is not proof of missing submission documents.
- **GCP-11 (changes/deviations):** Describe version control, approval/dissemination of material amendments, and deviations documentation relevant to the study.
